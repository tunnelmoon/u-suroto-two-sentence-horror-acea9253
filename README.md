# Two-Sentence Horror

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253).

A horror story told in exactly two sentences: a doorbell camera, a neighbour
leaving small gifts on a porch, and a second sentence that makes the first
one worse on a second read.

`main.ts` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`. The story is 179 characters
in exactly two sentences, well inside the 1 to 400 character gate.
